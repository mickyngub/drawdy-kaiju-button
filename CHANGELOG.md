## 1.0.0

First public release

- A kaiju button on the board: press it and a kaiju walks across your view, leaving a footprint at every step
- Every stomp jolts the view and knocks nearby elements away, and the closest ones fly
- A last roar shakes everything harder at the end
- Only you see it: the kaiju and footprints are an overlay on your screen, and stomped elements are previews that never sync or enter undo
- Nothing on the board changes: when it ends, every element is back where it was and the footprints are gone
- Press the button again or Escape to stop it early
